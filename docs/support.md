# Support & Sponsorship

Audience: operators.

Part of the [Performance Monitor documentation](../README.md#documentation).

Performance Monitor is free and open source under the MIT License. Every user gets the same tool, with the same collectors and the same MCP server, and no features are withheld. The tools that it replaces charge thousands of dollars per server per year. A support subscription costs $500 a year and covers all of your SQL Server instances.

## What a subscription gets you

- When collection stops or a number looks wrong, you email Darling Data, the company that builds the tool, and you get an answer.
- When a new SQL Server version or cumulative update ships, the tool keeps working with it. Compatibility updates are guaranteed.
- Your feature requests go to the front of the queue.
- Your purchasing, compliance and audit teams get a support agreement and invoices, so the tool can go into production.
- You never count instances. Both tiers cover unlimited instances.

Priority adds four things to the list above. You get answers by the next business day, a live Q&A session each quarter, early access to new features, and direct input on the roadmap.

| Tier | Cost per year | Email response | Includes |
|------|---------------|----------------|----------|
| **Supported** | $500 | Within 2 business days | Guaranteed compatibility updates, priority feature requests, a support agreement and invoices, unlimited instances |
| **Priority** | $2,500 | By the next business day | Everything in Supported, plus a quarterly live Q&A session, early access to new features, and direct input on the roadmap |

The software is the same in both tiers and in the free download. You pay for the support, not for features.
